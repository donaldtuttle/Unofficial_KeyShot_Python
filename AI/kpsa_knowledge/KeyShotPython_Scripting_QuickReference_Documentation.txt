## Math

- `luxmath.Matrix` — `makeIdentity`, `makeTranslate`, `makeRotate`, `makeScale`, `rotateAroundAxis`
- `luxmath.Vector` — `(x, y, z)`, a single number, or a 3-tuple

## Materials

- `lux.getMaterialGraph(name)`
- `lux.getSceneMaterials()`
- A scene node uses `node.getMaterial()`
- OpenPBR and Planar Light parameters are in `AI/materials/`. Inspect the graph.
