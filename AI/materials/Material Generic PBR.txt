### Material PARAMETER_TYPE: Generic Material
  - **SHADER_TYPE**: lux_generic
  - **anisotropic** (PARAMETER_TYPE_FLOAT): Degree of anisotropy. (Value: 0.0)
  - **anisotropic direction** (PARAMETER_TYPE_INTEGER): Direction of anisotropy. (Value: 0)
  - **anisotropic_angle** (PARAMETER_TYPE_FLOAT): Angle of anisotropy. (Value: 0.0)
  - **apply_bump_to_labels** (PARAMETER_TYPE_BOOLEAN): Determines if bump maps are applied to labels. (Value: False)
  - **base_color** (PARAMETER_TYPE_COLOR): Base color of the material. (Value: (0.5, 0.5, 0.5, 1.0))
  - **base_color_map** (PARAMETER_TYPE_TEXTURE): Texture map for base color. (Value: Pure Parameter)
  - **bumpmap** (PARAMETER_TYPE_TEXTURE): Bump map texture. (Value: Pure Parameter)
  - **clearcoat** (PARAMETER_TYPE_FLOAT): Clearcoat layer intensity. (Value: 0.0)
  - **clearcoat_roughness** (PARAMETER_TYPE_FLOAT): Roughness of the clearcoat. (Value: 0.0)
  - **displacement** (PARAMETER_TYPE_TEXTURE): Displacement map. (Value: Pure Parameter)
  - **displacement_height** (PARAMETER_TYPE_FLOAT): Displacement height. (Value: 0.0)
  - **emissive** (PARAMETER_TYPE_COLOR): Emissive color. (Value: (0.0, 0.0, 0.0, 1.0))
  - **emissive_map** (PARAMETER_TYPE_TEXTURE): Emissive texture map. (Value: Pure Parameter)
  - **metallic** (PARAMETER_TYPE_FLOAT): Metallic factor (0 dielectric, 1 metal). (Value: 0.0)
  - **metallic_map** (PARAMETER_TYPE_TEXTURE): Metallic texture map. (Value: Pure Parameter)
  - **normal_map** (PARAMETER_TYPE_TEXTURE): Normal map texture. (Value: Pure Parameter)
  - **opacity** (PARAMETER_TYPE_FLOAT): Opacity of the material. (Value: 1.0)
  - **opacity_map** (PARAMETER_TYPE_TEXTURE): Opacity texture map. (Value: Pure Parameter)
  - **roughness** (PARAMETER_TYPE_FLOAT): Surface roughness. (Value: 0.5)
  - **roughness_map** (PARAMETER_TYPE_TEXTURE): Roughness texture map. (Value: Pure Parameter)
  - **specular** (PARAMETER_TYPE_FLOAT): Specular intensity. (Value: 0.5)
  - **specular_color** (PARAMETER_TYPE_COLOR): Specular color. (Value: (1.0, 1.0, 1.0, 1.0))
  - **specular_map** (PARAMETER_TYPE_TEXTURE): Specular texture map. (Value: Pure Parameter)
  - **use_base_color_map** (PARAMETER_TYPE_BOOLEAN): Enable base color map. (Value: False)
  - **use_emissive_map** (PARAMETER_TYPE_BOOLEAN): Enable emissive map. (Value: False)
  - **use_metallic_map** (PARAMETER_TYPE_BOOLEAN): Enable metallic map. (Value: False)
  - **use_normal_map** (PARAMETER_TYPE_BOOLEAN): Enable normal map. (Value: False)
  - **use_opacity_map** (PARAMETER_TYPE_BOOLEAN): Enable opacity map. (Value: False)
  - **use_roughness_map** (PARAMETER_TYPE_BOOLEAN): Enable roughness map. (Value: False)
  - **use_specular_map** (PARAMETER_TYPE_BOOLEAN): Enable specular map. (Value: False)
