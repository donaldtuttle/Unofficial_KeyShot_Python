### Material: Metal
  - **SHADER_TYPE**: lux_metal
  - **apply_bump_to_labels** (BOOLEAN): Determines if bump maps are applied to labels. (Value: False)
  - **bumpmap** (PARAMETER_TYPE_TEXTURE): Defines the bump map used for the material. (Value: Pure Parameter)
  - **coated** (PARAMETER_TYPE_BOOLEAN): Determines if the material is coated, affecting its reflective properties. (Value: False)
  - **color** (PARAMETER_TYPE_COLOR): The color of the material. (Value: (0.3025, 0.3025, 0.3025, 1.0))
  - **film_extinction** (PARAMETER_TYPE_FLOAT): Extinction coefficient for thin film interference. (Value: 0.0)
  - **film_ior** (PARAMETER_TYPE_FLOAT): Index of Refraction for thin film interference. (Value: 2.0)
  - **film_thickness** (PARAMETER_TYPE_FLOAT): Thickness of thin film interference. (Value: 100.0)
  - **glossy_samples** (PARAMETER_TYPE_INTEGER): Number of samples used for glossy reflections. (Value: 9)
  - **metal_preset** (PARAMETER_TYPE_INTEGER): Preset value for metal type. (Value: 1)
  - **metal_type** (PARAMETER_TYPE_INTEGER): Type of metal, affecting its overall appearance. (Value: 0)
  - **opacitymap** (PARAMETER_TYPE_TEXTURE): Defines the material's opacity. (Value: Pure Parameter)
  - **opacitymap_mode** (PARAMETER_TYPE_INTEGER): Mode for opacity map. (Value: 2)
  - **roughness** (PARAMETER_TYPE_FLOAT): Controls the sharpness of reflections. (Value: 0.0)
  - **spectral_ior** (PARAMETER_TYPE_ARRAY): Array defining the spectral Index of Refraction. (Value: [])

## The Metal material type is a simple material that simulates metals.
The color of the metal can be controlled by the color parameter, or by using a metal preset.
The roughness controls how sharp the reflections are.
The coated parameter adds a clear coat on top of the metal.
The thin film parameters (film_ior, film_thickness, film_extinction) can be used to simulate thin film interference effects on the metal surface.
The spectral_ior can be used for more accurate spectral rendering of the metal.
For all the metal presets, even if anodized is enabled or not, the color can depend on the viewing angle (just as with the color of real-world metals). You will notice slight color tints at grazing angles, especially for Gold and Aluminum.
